A postgraduate degree in mathematics is an opportunity to develop deeper understanding, explore areas that interest you and prepare for further academic or professional study. Alongside IIT JAM and CUET-PG, investigate mathematics programmes at **state universities and their recognised affiliated colleges**.

This guide gives particular attention to **Karnataka**, with a selective list of well-known state universities elsewhere in India and admission routes in neighbouring states. It is a starting point for comparing programmes, preparing effectively and completing applications carefully.

**Last reviewed: 23 September 2026.** Links include current portals and clearly labelled historical papers. Their presence does not mean applications are currently open. Consult the notification for your intended admission year.

<h2 id="admission-routes">How Do Admissions Work?</h2>

**There is no single admission procedure covering every state university.** Begin with the university's current PG notification and the specific Mathematics programme entry.

Depending on the programme, selection may use an entrance examination, qualifying-degree marks, or a combination. A shared state entrance test applies only to its participating institutions and listed courses. Some state universities also participate in CUET-PG; check the current programme mapping before choosing a test.

Official examples illustrate the differences:

- [Mangalore University's Mathematics course page](https://www.mangaloreuniversity.ac.in/course-intake-mathematics) describes a university entrance examination. Confirm its current operation through the annual admission notice.
- [University of Mysore's PG portal](https://www.uompgadmissions.com/) provides admission instructions, selection information and an archive of entrance papers.
- [Telangana CPGET](https://cpget.tgche.ac.in/) serves a specified group of participating universities.
- [University of Kerala's admissions portal](https://admissions.keralauniversity.ac.in/) separates PG admissions in affiliated colleges from PG-CSS admissions in university teaching departments.

**Do not infer eligibility from an exam's abbreviation.** Check the actual course list, subject paper and institution. In particular, similarly named PGCET and PGECET portals can refer to different examinations.

<h2 id="kud">Karnatak University: A Starting Point for Dharwad Students</h2>

Students at Karnatak Science College, Dharwad can begin by reviewing KUD's Mathematics admission notices and discussing their study plans with their teachers.

The [KUD admission-notice search](https://kud.ac.in/search_list.php?sh=admission) includes **2026–27 Mathematics provisional rank-list and counselling-vacancy notices**. These illustrate why students need to follow departmental updates after submitting an application; a general university announcement is only the beginning.

For each admission cycle, locate:

1. The first-year M.Sc. Mathematics notification and application instructions.
2. Eligibility and the stated method of preparing the merit list.
3. Entrance syllabus, date and instructions, if an entrance test is prescribed.
4. The seat matrix identifying the university department, PG centres and participating colleges.
5. Provisional lists, correction procedures and counselling schedules.
6. Fee-payment instructions and later vacancy rounds.

**Check the location attached to each seat.** A university department, a PG centre and an affiliated college may have different facilities, fees and application arrangements.

<h2 id="eligibility">Eligibility and Choosing a Programme</h2>

Check these items separately for every programme:

| Item | Questions to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Qualifying degree | Is your exact B.Sc. degree or subject combination accepted? |
| Mathematics background | Is Mathematics required as a major, optional subject or for a minimum number of semesters/credits? |
| Marks | Is the threshold based on Mathematics marks, overall aggregate, or another specified calculation? |
| Degree structure | How are three-year and four-year undergraduate degrees treated? What is the applicable PG duration? |
| Final-year candidates | Can you apply before results, and by what date must you submit proof of completion? |
| Selection | Is there an entrance test? What weighting, if any, is given to undergraduate marks? |
| Category / domicile | Which rules and certificate formats apply to your application? |
| Programme and campus | Is the seat for regular Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, another specialisation or an affiliated college? |

There is no universal percentage threshold or score formula to apply across this directory. Obtain written clarification from the relevant admission office if your degree pattern is not explicitly covered.

### Compare the academic experience

Read the curriculum rather than choosing only by the university name. Consider the balance of pure and applied mathematics, electives, project opportunities, computational work and access to seminars. Ask about the library, teaching arrangements, assessment methods and student support.

Make your shortlist manageable: include programmes you strongly prefer and other suitable options that fit your eligibility and budget.

These are historical practice papers, not the current examination instructions. Use the archive page if a direct link moves.

### Other official sources

- **KUD:** Check the [university notices](https://kud.ac.in/search_list.php?sh=admission) and Mathematics department announcements for any prescribed entrance syllabus and released papers.
- **TG CPGET:** Use the **Syllabus** and **Exam Pattern** links on the [official portal](https://cpget.tgche.ac.in/).
- **AP PGCET:** Use the [syllabus directory](https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in/PGCET/PGCET_HomePages/Syllabus.aspx). The [main portal](https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in/PGCET/PGCET/PGCET_HomePage.aspx) also provides **Master Question Papers with Preliminary Keys**. Check for final corrections before scoring your work.
- **Other universities:** Start from the current PG admission notice or ask the department whether official sample/past papers are available.

A B.Sc. semester examination paper is useful for revision but is not automatically a PG entrance paper. Likewise, an M.Sc. semester syllabus describes what you will study after admission, not necessarily what the entrance examination tests.

### Use past papers actively

1. Read the instructions for that paper's year: duration, number of questions and marking rules.
2. Attempt it before consulting solutions.
3. Match answers with the correct paper and official key, where available.
4. Classify mistakes as concept gaps, calculation errors, misreading or time-management problems.
5. Reattempt difficult questions a few days later.

Do not copy the CUET-PG marking scheme into a university entrance mock unless that university explicitly uses the same scheme.

<h2 id="preparation">Preparation and Free Learning Resources</h2>

### Build a common foundation, then specialise

The table below is a suggested undergraduate revision framework, **not a common official syllabus**. Tick only the topics prescribed by each target examination and add any missing areas.

| Area | Suggested revision |
| --- | --- |
| Calculus | Limits, continuity, differentiation, integration and multivariable methods |
| Linear algebra | Systems of equations, matrices, vector spaces, linear maps and eigenvalues |
| Real analysis | Sequences, series, convergence, theorem hypotheses and counterexamples |
| Abstract algebra | Groups, rings, fields and standard examples |
| Differential equations | First-order methods and linear equations |
| Complex analysis | Analyticity, integration and other topics named in the target syllabus |
| Additional topics | Number theory, vector calculus, numerical methods, probability or operations research, where prescribed |

### Free resources

| Resource | Suggested use |
| --- | --- |
| [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in/courses) | Find undergraduate mathematics lectures for topics you need to strengthen. |
| [SWAYAM](https://swayam.gov.in/) | Follow structured courses; check the course page for optional certification charges. |
| [MIT OpenCourseWare: Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/) | Connect matrix calculations with underlying concepts. |
| [MIT OpenCourseWare: Real Analysis](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-100a-real-analysis-fall-2020/) | Practise rigorous reasoning and proofs. |
| [Paul's Online Math Notes](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/) | Work through calculus and differential-equation examples. |
| [MTTS 4D Space](https://4dspace.mtts.org.in/ea) | Read expository articles and strengthen foundations. |
| [Online Courses on this website]({{ '/resources/online-courses/' | relative_url }}) | Explore additional learning links and lecture channels. |

A useful study routine is to review one concept, solve a small set of questions independently and correct your errors the same day. Keep a separate notebook of definitions, useful examples and mistakes you do not want to repeat.

### Suggested eight-week framework

- **Weeks 1–2:** shortlist universities, read their requirements and diagnose weaknesses using a past paper.
- **Weeks 3–4:** revise core topics and practise problems daily.
- **Weeks 5–6:** cover university-specific topics and attempt mixed question sets.
- **Weeks 7–8:** practise timed papers, review errors and finish pending application tasks.

Adapt this framework to your starting level and the actual examination schedule. If selection uses undergraduate marks, continue giving your semester coursework and examinations proper attention.

<h2 id="applications">Applications, Merit Lists and Counselling</h2>

### Documents to prepare

Use this as a planning list, then follow the exact notification. Requirements differ.

- Identification, photograph and signature in the specified formats.
- Undergraduate semester marks cards and degree/provisional certificate, when required.
- Prescribed conversion formula or certificate for CGPA, if applicable.
- Category, income, domicile, disability or other certificates relevant to your claim.
- Entrance score/rank card, where required.
- Transfer, migration or eligibility certificates at the stage specified by the institution.
- Application confirmation and payment receipts.

### After submitting an application

Monitor provisional merit lists and correction windows. Attend counselling or submit online choices as instructed. Check whether a seat has actually been allotted, accept it within the deadline, pay through the authorised process and complete verification. Save the admission confirmation.

Later vacancy rounds may provide another opportunity, but do not assume they will occur or that an earlier application automatically covers them.

### Understanding cut-offs

Compare only like-for-like information: the same programme, campus, year, category, admission round and selection formula. An entrance score, a degree percentage and a combined merit score are different measures. A previous closing score is a reference point, not an assurance of admission.

<h2 id="costs">Fees, Accommodation and Financial Support</h2>

Compare the **total cost of study**, including tuition, university/examination charges, deposits, hostel, mess, transport and study materials. Check whether a seat has a regular or self-financing fee structure.

Ask each university about the current scholarship and fee-concession schemes for which you may qualify, their deadlines and the required documents. Treat financial support and hostel accommodation as separate applications unless the institution explicitly states otherwise. Do not budget on an unconfirmed award or hostel allotment.

<h2 id="faqs">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<details>
<summary><strong>Must I write CUET-PG for every state university?</strong></summary>
<p>No. Check each programme's current admission notice. Some institutions use their own or a shared entrance examination; others may use qualifying marks or a combination. Use CUET-PG only where the programme accepts the relevant paper.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I use the same preparation for several universities?</strong></summary>
<p>Yes, where the syllabi overlap. Keep a shared topic checklist and a separate list for each examination's extra topics, format and marking rules.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Are university-department and affiliated-college applications always the same?</strong></summary>
<p>No. Check the exact institution and seat category. The University of Kerala portal, for example, has separate entry points for teaching-department and affiliated-college PG admissions.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can a student from Karnataka apply in another state?</strong></summary>
<p>Check the programme's degree-recognition and local/non-local eligibility rules. Seat availability, category treatment and fees may differ. Ask the official admission office if the brochure does not resolve your case.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Should I apply only to one university?</strong></summary>
<p>Consider several suitable programmes within your eligibility and budget. Compare academic fit and costs, and maintain a deadline tracker so that parallel applications remain manageable.</p>
</details>

## A Message to Students

A strong mathematical education grows through regular practice, careful reasoning and the willingness to ask questions. Choose a programme that supports your interests and circumstances. Make good use of your undergraduate years, speak with your teachers and explore more than one route to postgraduate study.
